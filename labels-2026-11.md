## Definitions to keep apart

- `feature`: new capability. `enhancement`: improvement to something that exists.
- `question`: how to use. `troubleshooting`: cause not established. `bug`: confirmed defect in motionEye.
- `external bug`: caused by software outside motionEye.
- `Home Assistant addon` vs `Home Assistant integration`: the add-on vs the integration built into HA.
